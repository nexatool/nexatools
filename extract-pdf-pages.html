What Is Extracting PDF Pages?

Extracting pages from a PDF means pulling out a chosen set of pages from a larger document and saving them as a brand new, separate PDF file. Instead of sending someone an entire 40 page report when they only need one section, you can extract just the pages that matter and share a smaller, focused file.

This is different from splitting a PDF into equal parts extraction lets you cherry-pick exact page numbers or ranges, in any order you like, and combine them into a single output file. It's one of the most useful everyday PDF tasks, whether you're pulling a signature page out of a contract or grabbing a single chapter from a long ebook.

When you upload a file into the tool above, it is read directly by your browser's own file-reading APIs and processed with JavaScript running on your device.
