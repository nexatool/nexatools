What Is Image Flipping?

Image flipping creates a mirror image of a photo or graphic, reflecting it across a horizontal or vertical axis. A horizontal flip swaps left and right, like looking in a mirror. A vertical flip swaps top and bottom, like turning the image upside down along its width. Flipping is different from rotating: a rotation turns the whole image around its center, while a flip reflects it, which can reverse text and asymmetric details differently than a rotation would.

NexaTools Image Flipper redraws your image onto an HTML5 canvas with the chosen mirror transform applied, entirely inside your browser.

Tips for Getting the Best Results

1. Watch for reversed text

Flipping mirrors everything in the frame, including any text or logos in the shot. Double check the preview if your image contains text.

2. Combine both directions for a 180° effect

Toggling horizontal and vertical flip together produces the same visual result as rotating the image 180° useful if you only need a quick upside-down mirror.

3. Dimensions never change

Unlike rotation, flipping never changes the width or height of your image, so it's a safe option when you need to preserve exact dimensions.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

Running into an issue while flipping your image? Here are fixes for the most common problems:

⚠️ Flip doesn't look like anything changed

Perfectly symmetrical images can look the same after a flip. Check the live preview closely, or look for any text in the image to confirm the mirror direction.

⚠️ Image looks upside down instead of mirrored

You may have vertical flip enabled instead of horizontal. Toggle the direction buttons to switch between them.

⚠️ "Could not flip this image"

The file may be corrupted or in an unsupported format. Confirm it's a valid JPG, PNG, or WebP file and try again.

⚠️ File larger than 20 MB

Very large images aren't supported in-browser. Try compressing the image first, then flip it.

⚠️ Live preview not updating

Try toggling a flip button off and back on the preview refreshes automatically on any change.

⚠️ Download button doesn't work

Some browsers block automatic downloads from JavaScript. Check your browser's download permissions or pop-up settings for this site.
