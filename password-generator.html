What Is a Password Generator?

A password generator produces random, high-entropy credentials instead of relying on you to invent one from memory. Left to guess on our own, most people pick something predictable a pet's name, a birth year, a favorite team plus a couple of digits. It feels personal, but automated cracking tools have already seen millions of variations just like it. A generator removes that predictability by drawing characters, words, or digits at random, so there's no dictionary or guessing script that can reliably reproduce it.

Every account you hold email, banking, shopping, work tools becomes a target the moment its password is weak or reused elsewhere. When one site suffers a data breach, attackers immediately try the leaked password on other services. A unique password per account keeps that damage contained to a single login instead of cascading across your whole digital life.

Password Security Tips

Use a Different Password for Every Account

If one website gets breached and you reused that password, every other account sharing it is also at risk. Always generate a unique password per account.

Use a Password Manager

You do not need to memorize strong passwords. A trusted password manager such as Bitwarden, 1Password, or Dashlane stores them securely never a text file, notes app, or spreadsheet.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Even a strong password can be stolen in a breach. Turn on 2FA for important accounts like email, banking, and social media as an extra layer of protection.

Favor Length Over Complexity

A longer password is generally harder to crack than a short one stuffed with symbols. Where a site allows it, favor 16+ characters or a 4-6 word passphrase.

Privacy First By Design

Every password NexaTools generates is created entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript running on your device. Nothing is transmitted to a server, nothing is logged, and nothing is stored not the passwords themselves, and no unnecessary personal data is collected. Once you navigate away from the page, the generated passwords exist only wherever you chose to save or paste them.
