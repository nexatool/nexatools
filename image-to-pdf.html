What Is Image to PDF Conversion?

Converting images to PDF places one or more pictures onto pages inside a single PDF document, in the order you choose. It's the standard way to turn a handful of photos say, scanned pages, receipts, or a photo series into one file that's easy to share, print, or archive, instead of sending several separate image files.

NexaTools Image to PDF Converter builds the PDF file directly inside your browser using JavaScript. Each image you upload becomes its own page, and you can drag the order around before converting.

NexaTools Image to PDF Converter avoids that entirely. Tips for Getting the Best Results

1. Upload images in roughly the order you want

While you can reorder pages with the ▲ ▼ buttons, starting with files already close to your desired order saves a few clicks.

2. Use "Fit Image" for mixed-size pictures

If your images have very different aspect ratios, "Fit Image" avoids extra white margins by matching each page exactly to its picture's dimensions.

3. Use A4 or Letter for a document-style PDF

For a PDF meant to look like a traditional document or be printed, A4 or Letter centers each image on a consistent standard page size.

4. Keep image count reasonable per batch

Very large batches of high-resolution photos take longer to process since everything runs in your browser split into smaller batches if needed.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

Running into an issue while converting your images? Here are fixes for the most common problems:

⚠️ "Could not read this image"

The file may be corrupted or in an unsupported format. Confirm it's a valid JPG, PNG, or WebP file and try again.

⚠️ Pages are in the wrong order

Use the ▲ ▼ buttons next to each thumbnail in the list to move pages up or down before converting.

⚠️ File larger than 20 MB

Very large images aren't supported in-browser. Try compressing the image first, then add it to your PDF.

⚠️ PDF looks too zoomed in or has large margins

Switch the page size option to "Fit Image" so each page matches its picture exactly, instead of centering it on a fixed A4 or Letter page.

⚠️ Conversion is slow

A large number of high-resolution images takes longer to encode since everything runs locally. Try converting in smaller batches.

⚠️ Download button doesn't work

Some browsers block automatic downloads from JavaScript. Check your browser's download permissions or pop-up settings for this site.
