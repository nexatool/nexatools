Free Online PDF Merger

The NexaTools Merge PDF tool combines two or more separate PDF files into a single document, with pages in whatever order you choose. Instead of sending five separate attachments, you end up with one clean file that opens with a single click.

The whole process runs locally on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your files are never sent to a server, which makes it a safe option for contracts, invoices, ID scans, or anything else you'd rather not upload anywhere.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

"Merge PDFs" button stays disabled. It only activates once at least two valid PDF files have been added check that both appear in the list above.

⚠️

"PDF engine failed to load" error. The merging library didn't load in your browser check your connection and refresh the page.

⚠️

Merge fails with an error. This usually means one of the files is corrupted or password-protected remove the password and try again.

⚠️

A file was skipped on upload. Files over 25 MB, or files that aren't valid PDFs, are skipped automatically with a warning message explaining why.
