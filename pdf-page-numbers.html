What Does Adding Page Numbers to a PDF Mean?

Adding page numbers means printing a small number, usually at the bottom or top corner of each page, so readers can track their place and reference specific pages easily. It's one of the simplest formatting touches that makes a long document feel organized and professional, whether it's a report, ebook, thesis, or legal filing.

Page numbers become especially useful once a document is printed or shared as a standalone PDF, where there's no on-screen scroll position to rely on. A quick "Page 12 of 40" at the bottom of a page tells the reader exactly where they are and how much is left.
