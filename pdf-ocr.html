What Is PDF OCR?

OCR stands for optical character recognition, a technology that analyzes an image of text and converts it into machine-readable, selectable text. When a PDF is made from a scanned document or a photo, the "text" on the page is really just a picture; you can't select, search, or copy it the normal way. OCR solves that by reading the shapes of the letters and turning them back into actual text characters.

This tool renders each page of your PDF as an image and then runs OCR on it directly in your browser, producing plain text you can copy or download.
