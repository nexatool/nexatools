What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a simple utility that analyzes a block of text and reports how many words, characters, sentences and paragraphs it contains, along with an estimated reading time. It's the tool writers, students and marketers reach for whenever a piece of content needs to fit a specific length limit.

The NexaTools Word Counter updates live as you type or paste, entirely inside your browser. Nothing you write is uploaded to a server, which matters when you're drafting something private, like an unpublished article or a client document.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

My count seems off from another tool. Different tools count hyphenated words, numbers or bullet points slightly differently; small variations between tools are normal.

⚠️

Copy button doesn't seem to work. Some browsers block clipboard access until you interact with the page first; click anywhere on the page, then try Copy Text again.

⚠️

Reading time looks too fast or slow. The estimate uses an average reading speed; technical or dense text will realistically take longer to read than the estimate shows.
