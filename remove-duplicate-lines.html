What Is a Duplicate Line Remover?

A duplicate line remover scans a block of text line by line and deletes any repeated entries, keeping only the first occurrence of each unique line in its original order. It's a fast way to clean up messy lists, exported data, or copy-pasted content.

The NexaTools Remove Duplicate Lines tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded to a server, so it's safe to use on internal lists, email addresses, or any content you'd rather keep private.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

Some lines that look identical weren't removed. Check the case-sensitive option; "Apple" and "apple" are only merged when case-sensitive matching is turned off.

⚠️

Lines with extra spaces weren't matched. Enable "Trim whitespace before comparing" so leading and trailing spaces don't count as differences.

⚠️

Copy button doesn't seem to work. Some browsers block clipboard access until you interact with the page first; click anywhere on the page, then try Copy Result again.
