What Is This Invoice Generator?

The NexaTools Invoice Generator is a browser-based tool that turns a short form into a finished, ready-to-send invoice. Fill in who's billing whom, list the services or products provided, and the tool calculates subtotals, tax, and grand totals automatically so nothing is left to manual arithmetic.

It's built with freelancers and small businesses in mind, especially those working across borders. You can bill in US dollars while showing a live PKR equivalent, apply GST at the standard 17% rate or any custom percentage, and attach the exact payment details your client needs whether that's a JazzCash number, an Easypaisa wallet, a Payoneer email, or a standard bank transfer.

Because everything runs locally in your browser, there's no account to create and no server processing your business details. You fill in the form, watch the preview update instantly, and download a polished PDF when you're ready.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

The PKR equivalent isn't showing. Make sure USD (or another non-PKR currency) is selected and an exchange rate is entered.

⚠️

GST isn't calculating. Check the Apply GST box the default 17% rate only applies once it's enabled.

⚠️

The PDF didn't download. After clicking Print / Save as PDF, choose Save as PDF as the destination in your browser's print dialog.

⚠️

Payment details aren't printing. Confirm you clicked the relevant payment method button and entered your account details in the field that appeared.
