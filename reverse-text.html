What Is a Reverse Text Tool?

A reverse text tool flips your text in one of several ways: it can spell everything backwards character by character, flip the order of the words while keeping each word spelled correctly, or reverse the order of the lines in a list. Each mode produces a very different result from the same input.

The NexaTools Reverse Text tool processes everything locally in your browser. Nothing you type or paste is uploaded to a server, so it's safe to use for private notes, code, or drafts.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

My result looks scrambled, not reversed. Check which mode you selected; character reversal looks very different from word or line reversal.

⚠️

An emoji or special character looks odd after reversing. Character-by-character reversal can occasionally split multi-part emoji or accented characters visually.

⚠️

Copy button doesn't seem to work. Some browsers block clipboard access until you interact with the page first; click anywhere on the page, then try Copy Result again.
