What Is an Image Watermark?

A watermark is text or a logo overlaid onto an image, usually semi-transparent, to mark ownership, brand a photo, or discourage unauthorized use. Photographers, businesses, and content creators add watermarks before sharing images publicly so their work stays identifiable even if it's copied or reshared elsewhere.

NexaTools Image Watermark Tool draws your chosen text or logo directly onto an HTML5 canvas at the position, size, color and opacity you set, entirely inside your browser.

Tips for Getting the Best Results

1. Use moderate opacity

A watermark around 50 70% opacity is visible enough to identify your work without overwhelming the image underneath.

2. Corner placements are less intrusive

Bottom-right or bottom-left positions keep the watermark visible while staying out of the main subject of most photos.

3. Use a transparent PNG for logo watermarks

A logo with a transparent background blends into the image naturally, while a solid background will show as a visible box.

4. Match text color to your image

Light text works best on darker images and dark text on lighter images use the color picker to keep it readable.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

Running into an issue while adding a watermark? Here are fixes for the most common problems:

⚠️ Watermark is hard to see

Increase the opacity slider, or switch to a text color that contrasts more with your image.

⚠️ Logo shows a solid box around it

Your logo file likely doesn't have a transparent background. Use a PNG logo with transparency for a cleaner result.

⚠️ Watermark looks too large or too small

Adjust the Size slider it scales the text or logo relative to the image dimensions.

⚠️ "Could not process this image"

The file may be corrupted or in an unsupported format. Confirm it's a valid JPG, PNG, or WebP file and try again.

⚠️ File larger than 20 MB

Very large images aren't supported in-browser. Try compressing the image first, then add the watermark.

⚠️ Download button doesn't work

Some browsers block automatic downloads from JavaScript. Check your browser's download permissions or pop-up settings for this site.
