What Is a Character Counter?

A character counter tracks exactly how many characters, letters, numbers, symbols and spaces a block of text contains. Unlike word counting, character limits matter for platforms with hard technical caps, such as a tweet, an SMS message, or a search engine's meta description snippet.

The NexaTools Character Counter updates live as you type, with preset limits for common platforms so you can see at a glance how much room is left before you hit a cutoff.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️

My count doesn't match the target platform exactly. Some platforms count emoji, links or line breaks differently; use this tool as a close estimate rather than an exact match for every platform's internal counting rules.

⚠️

The bar turned red. That means your text is over the selected preset's limit; trim it down until the bar returns to blue.

⚠️

Copy button doesn't seem to work. Some browsers block clipboard access until you interact with the page first; click anywhere on the page, then try Copy Text again.
