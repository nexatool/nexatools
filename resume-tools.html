How to Build an ATS-Friendly Resume

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan resumes before a human ever sees them. Keeping your resume simple, well-structured and keyword-relevant helps it pass those automated filters and reach a hiring manager. A few practical habits make a real difference:

  • Keep your resume to one or two pages.
  • Tailor it for each job application.
  • Use clear section headings.
  • Highlight measurable achievements.
  • Save as PDF before submitting.

Common Problems & Troubleshooting

⚠️ PDF export looks different than the preview

Some fonts render slightly differently between screen and PDF; zoom into the exported PDF to confirm spacing before sending it.

⚠️ AI summary feels generic

Add more specific details — job titles, tools, achievements — before generating; the summary quality depends on what you provide.

⚠️ Layout looks cramped

Trim longer bullet points or switch to a template with more whitespace if a section looks crowded.

⚠️ Lost my progress

Since nothing is saved to a server, avoid closing the tab mid-edit; finish and export your PDF before navigating away.
